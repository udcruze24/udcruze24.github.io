### 🧬 Executive Summary

Data Scientist and Bioinformatics Developer with **7+ years of experience** architecting production-grade **transcriptomics pipelines**, **harmonized single-cell atlases**, and **spatial omics workflows** for therapeutic drug discovery. 

I specialize in bridging high-dimensional multi-modal biology (`10x Visium HD`, multiplexed spatial proteomics, `scRNA-seq`, `CITE-seq`, `ATAC-seq`) with deep learning (`PyTorch`, `scvi-tools`, generative models) and reproducible workflow engineering (`Nextflow DSL2`, `Snakemake`, `Docker`, `SLURM HPC`, `AWS Batch`).

Author of peer-reviewed deep learning research for therapeutic antibody library design in [**_Briefings in Bioinformatics_ (2025)**](https://doi.org/10.1093/bib/bbaf023).
